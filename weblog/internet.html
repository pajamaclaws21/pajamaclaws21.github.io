It is increasingly embarrassing to be online. This has been my experience for a few weeks. The use of writing on website, of digital culture, is increasingly out of touch with analogue real world. Internet can no longer augment reality because it has been so hijacked by capitalism. There are interesting groups that have interesting things to say about this. the institute of network cultures studies the culture of the internet, of the technocapitalists. the cults, though, have lost their mystique after I discovered the vast abrupt. The characterization of them, the seemingly sensible, as schizotypal really explains a lot of what I appreciate about this genre of internetdom. They link things and concepts as though they are their own net. This is what I appreciate and what I find so scary---esoteric knowledge---about the Rationalists and Zizians etc. You can, supposedly, trace VA down to the CCRU, who---apparently---are accelerationists in their own right. The existence and continued existence of the Internet should not be neglected, but Internet-nihilism is fanatical. It can't save us all, either; see the numerous theories of Network Cultures and Jane Rosenzwieg. The Rationalists are wrong about AI, even if it can be apocalyptic; they brought about such an apocalypse. The cure to which is de-digitizing. Techno-esotericism is boring. Adieu from the web.
