parfum

currently I have only a few fragrances. I love them dearly. I am a sucker for an acquatic scent because it matches the deodorant I like (old spice fiji). although now i wear old spice orchard. The apple one. Sigh.

  • 34 bohemian cafes by thin wild mercury
    • Review: smells like incense on me---for church. a bit smoky. acquired taste for me; I bought as a winter perfume, I haven't gotten much use of it until recent church events.
  • missing person by phlur
    • Review: musky skin scent i wear to sleep. popular, and for a good reason. not for out in public. it is comforting but personal. post-shower, winding-down
  • sydney rock pool by arquiste
    • Review: this is my daily perfume. a gift from my cowboy. love smelling it on my shirtcuffs, which is where it lasts most. it doesn't go long on my skin
  • 11 11 azure by lake & skye
    • Review: it smells divine. my favorite. it is too good to wear. it is summery and vacationey. it smells so good. i am a bit afraid to wear it for that
  • scottatura by gleam
    • Review: i had a 1ml decant and i can't afford another sample. want again. bad. supposed to smell like bal d'afrique. sigh.

largely these are travel sizes. (That 34 one was $50 hooolyy.) I do not have the disposable income to drop on new big bottles of perfume frequently. I wish. Sabes? Have been wanting some more molecular-skin-scent stuff so... maybe around Christmastide.
