Term Definition
Scary Something thalassophobic; of morbid curiosity; generally a mirror for an unsustainable human pattern; recalling ick; feels as though it could drag you in. See here for more.
Evil Definition pending
Deconstruction Deconstruction is an ex-Evangelical practice, originally, in which someone grapples with the problematic aspects of their former faith. They deconstruct what they were taught and what they believe in order to take what is good and leave the rest. See ego death for my own; this page for other links.
Glossary A home for ideas not large enough yet to merit their own page; an articulate manifesto; the more I work on them the more I think that it is an Impressionist fantasy of making things clearer by adding foggy layers of paint. And also things that are just interesting/important. Their repository. ¿Sabes? See here for more on the political nature of glossaries.
Pattern Something that repeats :~)
Something I think I take myself too seriously. I'm not a phemomenologist, am I?
Valentine filter CJ the X (taken from "The Neurosis of Cat Valentine"): textually, the process by which the character of Cat Valentine was reduced to a caricature of the original. Felt like executive-level misogyny, called her "lobotomy" by fans. I think this idea is applicable when we're talking about internalized misogyny, though, and masking---how many people (especially women) dumb themselves down/act ditzy just because they have a partner? Or just to be attractive? It is a symptom of patriarchysickness, sure, and of course it's sad. It also happens to be frustrating.
Simulacra Baudrillard: the copy with no original
Simulate Baudrillard: pretending you're not pretending
Dissimulate Baudrillard: pretending to be something
Solae (sola fide, sola scriptura, sola gratia) Protestant reformation: sola fide (faith alone provides salvation, not acts); sola scriptura (scripture alone is the Word of God, not the Pope etc.); sola gratia (we are saved through the grace alone, you can't earn it). See weblog/ego death.
God is not good I encountered this on the internet and will probably find a backing for someday. This encompasses the idea that God is fundamentally incomprehensible to us; the only things we can know about God are embodied in the negative. God is not Good because God goes beyond the human conception of Good---it is the closest approximate but it does not encompass. This can be controversial; the end of this train goes many different atheistic places. Then how can we know anything about God? for one. See weblog/ego death.
Tellism The religion of Mr. Brian Telles: love God & don't be an asshole. See /weblog/tellism for more.
Exhibitionism Flaunting what you've got.
Politics How things are related. A politic is a way in which things are related...
Art pure Maybe a word I made up, taken from a coworker who loves cínema pur. In my mind I use this to mean l'art pour l'art, which is better known to anglophones as art for art's sake. Originally used anti-Marx to say "art has no political function"---or didatic/moral, according to Wikipedia---in contrast to Marxists needing art to have a political function, lest it be bourgeois and useless. But there is something in-between. Art can have these functions---but it must not need to. We cannot judge art for the product alone; art itself has merit.
Science pure Similar in formation to art pure. From pure science, as defined by UC Berkeley's Paleontology Dept. here: Research undertaken to build knowledge and understanding, regardless of its potential applications. The boundary between pure and applied science is fuzzy. Research undertaken in the pure pursuit of knowledge often ends up having useful applications, and research begun with an application in mind often ends up informing our understanding of the natural world more broadly. In my view: rather than research for a necessary purpose, that research and scientific exploration is necessary to prevent stagnation and backwards movement; although some exploration is political, that science must not need be political, that science is necessary. Despite political spending and funding---and exhibitionism---we must not get rid of science! We cannot judge science based on its results alone, science itself has merit. Of course things to be said for the validity of sciences as a knowledge-maker but that goes beyond the scope of this definition.
