Deconstruction

Deconstruction is an ex-Evangelical practice, originally, in which someone grapples with the problematic aspects of their former faith. They deconstruct what they were taught and what they believe in order to take what is good and leave the rest. You can guess where this is headed.

Previously, on the cult page: Deconstruction is what ex-evangelicals call their critical analysis of their faith post-exit. I think this is rational. For such a freaky thing, something that screwed us over, how can we decide what we take with us into our daily lives and what to leave behind? How can we acknowledge etc? This is the stance I believe needs to be taken towards the cults. The true nature of what is terrifying is, as i established prior and in my glossary, I find myself on the other side of a mirror from them---as though one rogue thought and boom I'm in. This is the general terror of cults: they lie in the uncanny valley!! Deconstruction is important because for many people the cult contaminates the mainstream culture. This is how I feel about rationalism; that LessWrong and Roko's and the Zizians contaminated any good Yudowksy had to say. My friend Ink noted well that the people who take Yudowsky's word as sacred are the ones who corrupt it. For me, the culture of the rationalists corrupts the generally-okay idea of rationalism (generally we need to be more rational). For Ink this leads to post-rationalism (his deconstruction has the rational root as incontroverted) and for me it leads to Internet Esotericism (ha ha ha). That's a joke but not really, it is a conscious design choice I make. I try to show very clearly here that I feel on the other side of a mirror from these people, that I like computer. Etc; I try to type like them, I try to have this thing where I establish what I'm saying at various points throughout the essay. But the point is that for me, rationalism and the internet cults---hell everyone---are forever tainted by their philosophy. That is harsh. It is not how i generally feel. But welcome to the internet, baby. So here is my philosophy and in these words!! While once I would've become a rationalist, an AI hyper, I am forever divorced from them by my deconstruction. Never will I be a Luddite and forever will I subjugate the machine. Rationalism is too linked to tech bros to not die a hard death ... and maybe in the Sixth Republic we will have a fairer system.
